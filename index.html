Mingyan Yang

I am Mingyan Yang (杨茗焱), a senior undergraduate computer science student from ACM Honor Class, Zhiyuan College, Shanghai Jiao Tong University. Currently, I am a research intern advised by Prof. Jinyang Li at New York University. I also had the opportunity to work with Prof. Chen Chen at Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

My research interests lie at the intersection of machine learning systems and large-scale 3D representations, with a focus on efficient systems and algorithms for scalable scene rendering and neural representations.

I am applying to CS PhD programs for Fall 2026 and would be happy to connect—feel free to check my CV or reach out if you’re interested in my research.
